# Community conduct

SREF discussions require technical candour and professional respect.

Participants MUST:

- critique proposals and evidence rather than people;
- make room for different levels of language and standards experience;
- disclose relevant conflicts of interest;
- avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, and the
  publication of another person's private information; and
- follow maintainer direction when a discussion is moved, narrowed, or closed.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive material, restrict participation, or ban
participants whose conduct makes collaboration unsafe or persistently
unproductive. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented
privately, and made without retaliation.

Report conduct concerns privately to a project maintainer rather than debating
them in a public issue. If the concern involves a maintainer, contact another
maintainer through the hosting platform.
