# Governance

SREF has one maintainer.

## Roles

| Role        | Who                                       | Responsibilities                                                                                      |
| ----------- | ----------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Maintainer  | Jonathan la Cour ([@laCour][maintainer])  | Reviews and merges changes, publishes releases, answers security reports, administers the repository. |
| Contributor | Anyone who opens an issue or pull request | Submits changes under the terms in [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md).                              |

The maintainer is the only member with write or administrative access to this
repository and to the `savorum` GitHub organization that hosts it. The
organization requires two-factor authentication.

## Decisions

The maintainer decides what merges. A change to the specification also follows
the [change process](docs/change-process.md), and the maintainer may narrow a
proposal or move it to an extension as described in
[`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md#proposing-a-change). Conduct is governed by
the [code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

New maintainers are added by the existing maintainers and must meet the
organization's account requirements.

[maintainer]: https://github.com/laCour
